About this project

My Mission

Hi! My name is Daniel Wen, and I am a high school junior passionate about mathematics. I created this project because I believe math is far more interesting, creative, and meaningful than the way it is often taught in school. Through my own experiences learning outside the classroom, tutoring peers, and exploring mathematical ideas for fun, I saw how often students are pushed toward memorization, test preparation, and formula-based learning instead of genuine understanding.

This website is designed for middle and high school students who may be curious, confused, or simply not yet convinced that math is worth exploring. I want to make Calculus feel more intuitive, visual, and engaging by turning abstract concepts into interactive experiences.

While this project was born from my experiences in high school, it is designed for anyone with a curious mind. The goal of this website is not to replace a traditional calculus course, but to supplement it with interactive experiences that help learners notice patterns, ask better questions, and build conceptual intuition before diving into formal techniques. In a system where many students feel bored, overwhelmed, or disconnected from math, I want this project to be a place where learning feels exploratory rather than mechanical.

I also see this as part of a larger idea: education should not only teach people what to know, but also show them why it matters and help them enjoy learning in a way that lasts beyond the classroom. If this helps even a few students ignite or reignite their interest in math, then it has served its purpose.

Please note: This project is still a work in progress, and I am continuing to refine the lessons and expand the experience as new ideas are developed. The focus is always on making difficult ideas feel more accessible and intrinsically interesting.
